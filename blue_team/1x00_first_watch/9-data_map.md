# MedDefense Health Systems — Data Map

## Data Classification and Lifecycle Map

This data map identifies the major categories of information handled by MedDefense and traces how each category exists **at rest**, moves **in transit**, and is handled **in use**. Where the exact storage location, transmission method or protection is not documented, it is marked as unknown rather than assumed.

## Data Risk Summary

MedDefense's most significant data-protection weakness is the **systemic exposure of Restricted clinical information across a broadly reachable internal environment**, with controls that are much stronger at selected endpoints than across the complete data lifecycle. The widest protection gap is **patient medical-record data while in transit across the Central environment**: EHR data is highly sensitive, yet the database is reachable from the wider internal network, effective east-west segmentation is absent, and centralized monitoring is not in place to identify abnormal access or movement. The Task 3 unattended EHR session also shows that this exposure continues into the **data-in-use** state, where a technically protected record can still be disclosed simply because an authenticated session is left visible. Because weaknesses in transit and use affect Restricted information central to patient care, MedDefense should prioritize enforced segmentation, least-privilege access paths, stronger session controls and centralized monitoring rather than relying only on perimeter protection.
