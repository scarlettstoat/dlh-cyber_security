## 1. Purpose

This Acceptable Use Policy establishes requirements for the appropriate and secure use of TechSecure Solutions Inc. systems, networks, devices, applications, and information.

The purpose of this policy is to reduce security risks, protect confidential company information, support productivity, and ensure that company technology is used responsibly.

---

## 2. Scope

### 2.1 Applicability

This policy applies to:

- [x] All employees
- [x] Contractors and consultants
- [x] Third-party vendors with authorised access
- [x] Remote workers
- [x] Temporary staff and interns

### 2.2 Systems/Assets Covered

This policy applies to all company-owned or company-managed technology resources, including:

- Corporate email
- Company laptops and workstations
- Cloud-based development environments
- Corporate networks
- VPN services
- Collaboration and messaging tools
- Software development platforms
- Company data and files
- Approved mobile and personal devices used for work purposes

### 2.3 Exclusions

- Personal systems and services that are not used to access company data or systems are outside the scope of this policy.
- Any exception involving company systems or data must follow the exception process defined in Section 7.

---

## 3. Policy Statements

### 3.1 Internet Usage

Company internet access must primarily be used for legitimate business activities.

**Requirements:**

- Limited personal browsing is permitted when it does not interfere with work duties, consume excessive resources, or create security risks.
- Users must not access illegal, offensive, malicious, or inappropriate content using company systems.
- Users must not use company networks to download unauthorised or pirated material.
- Users must not attempt to bypass security controls, web filters, or monitoring systems.
- Internet activity on company systems may be monitored and logged for security, compliance, and operational purposes.
- Users should not expect complete privacy when using company-owned systems or networks.

### 3.2 Email Usage

Corporate email is provided primarily for business communication.

**Requirements:**

- Work email accounts must primarily be used for business purposes.
- Limited personal use is permitted only when it is occasional and does not interfere with work responsibilities.
- Users must not use corporate email to send offensive, illegal, fraudulent, or inappropriate content.
- Confidential or sensitive information must only be sent to authorised recipients.
- Users must verify recipients before sending sensitive information.
- Suspicious emails, phishing attempts, and unexpected attachments must be reported to the Information Security Team.
- Users must not automatically forward company email to personal email accounts.
- Email records must be retained according to company retention requirements and applicable legal or regulatory obligations.
- Users must not delete emails that are subject to a legal hold, investigation, or formal retention requirement.

### 3.3 Software Usage

Only authorised software may be installed or used on company systems.

**Requirements:**

- Employees must not install software without approval from IT.
- Software must be obtained from approved sources.
- Pirated, unlicensed, or illegally copied software is prohibited.
- Software installation requests must be submitted through the approved IT service process.
- IT must review software for licensing, compatibility, and security risks before installation.
- Users must not disable antivirus, endpoint protection, security agents, or system updates.
- Unauthorised software discovered on company devices may be removed without prior notice.

### 3.4 Social Media Usage

Employees must protect company information when using social media.

**Requirements:**

- Confidential, proprietary, or non-public project information must not be posted on social media.
- Employees must not share source code, screenshots, internal documents, customer information, or unreleased product details without authorisation.
- Employees must not represent personal opinions as official company statements.
- Only authorised employees may speak on behalf of TechSecure Solutions Inc.
- Employees who identify themselves as company employees should make it clear when opinions are personal.
- Social media activity must not violate company confidentiality, harassment, or conduct policies.
- Suspected disclosure of confidential company information must be reported immediately.

### 3.5 Bring Your Own Device (BYOD)

Personal devices may only connect to company systems or networks when authorised.

**Requirements:**

- Personal devices must be registered and approved by IT before accessing company resources.
- Devices must use a supported operating system and current security updates.
- Devices must be protected with a PIN, password, biometric control, or equivalent screen lock.
- Approved endpoint security or mobile device management controls must be installed where required.
- Company data must not be stored on unapproved applications or personal cloud storage services.
- Lost or stolen devices with access to company systems must be reported immediately.
- The company may remotely remove corporate data from authorised personal devices when necessary to protect company information.
- Rooted, jailbroken, or otherwise security-modified devices must not access company systems.
- Access may be revoked if a personal device no longer meets security requirements.

### 3.6 Data Handling

Company information must be stored, shared, and disposed of securely.

**Requirements:**

- Company data must only be stored in approved company systems or cloud services.
- Sensitive or confidential information must only be shared with authorised users who have a legitimate business need.
- Users must not upload company data to personal cloud storage, personal email accounts, or unauthorised file-sharing services.
- Access controls must be used to restrict sensitive information to authorised users.
- Confidential data must be encrypted when required by company standards.
- Physical documents containing confidential information must be securely stored when not in use.
- Electronic data must be securely deleted when no longer required according to company retention requirements.
- Paper records containing confidential information must be shredded or placed in approved secure disposal containers.
- Users must report suspected data loss, accidental sharing, or unauthorised disclosure immediately.

## 5. Compliance

### 5.1 Monitoring

TechSecure Solutions Inc. may monitor the use of company systems to protect company information and detect policy violations.

Monitoring may include:

- Internet browsing activity
- Corporate email activity
- Software installations
- Network and VPN logs
- Device connections
- Access to cloud services
- Security alerts and endpoint activity

Monitoring will be conducted for legitimate security, operational, legal, and compliance purposes.

### 5.2 Reporting

Employees must report suspected violations, security incidents, unauthorised software, lost devices, phishing attempts, or accidental data disclosure to the IT Service Desk or Information Security Team.

Serious or repeated violations will be reported to relevant management and Human Resources.

### 5.3 Auditing

The Information Security Team may perform periodic audits to confirm compliance with this policy.

Audits may review:

- Installed software
- BYOD registrations
- Access permissions
- Email and data handling practices
- Security logs
- Compliance with approved technology standards

Formal review of this policy will occur at least once every 12 months.

---

## 6. Enforcement

### 6.1 Violations

Violations of this policy may result in:

- Verbal warning
- Written warning
- Mandatory security awareness training
- Removal of unauthorised software
- Suspension of access privileges
- Removal of BYOD access
- Disciplinary action up to and including termination
- Legal action where applicable

The level of enforcement will depend on the seriousness, impact, and frequency of the violation.

### 6.2 Reporting Violations

Suspected violations must be reported to:

**Information Security Team or IT Service Desk**

Employees should report security concerns as soon as possible so that risks can be investigated and contained.

---

## 7. Exceptions

### 7.1 Exception Process

Exceptions to this policy require:

1. A written request to the Information Security Team
2. A clear business justification
3. A documented risk assessment
4. Compensating security controls where applicable
5. Formal approval and documentation

### 7.2 Exception Duration

All exceptions must have a defined end date and be reviewed at least annually.

High-risk exceptions may require more frequent review.
